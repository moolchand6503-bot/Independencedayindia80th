About Independence Day

India celebrates Independence Day every year on 15 August, remembering the end of British colonial rule in 1947 and honouring the freedom movement and its countless contributors.

🇮🇳 15 August 1947

India became independent after a long freedom struggle marked by courage, sacrifice and determination.

🕊️ Freedom

The day celebrates liberty, democracy, dignity and the responsibility to build a better India together.

☸ Unity

The Tiranga and Ashoka Chakra represent a nation united by shared values and hope for the future.

1857

The uprising of 1857 became a major early landmark in India's struggle against British rule.

1919–1942

Mass movements and public participation transformed the freedom struggle across India.

15 August 1947

India achieved independence. The first Prime Minister, Jawaharlal Nehru, raised the national flag at the Red Fort.
